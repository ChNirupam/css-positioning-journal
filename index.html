September 14, 2026

A Fresh Start on Campus

Today was a reminder that a new semester is more than a schedule of classes. It is a chance to meet new people, learn unfamiliar ideas, and build better habits one day at a time.

I walked across campus with a full list of tasks, but I also made time to notice the small things: the busy walkways, the quiet study spaces, and the feeling that every week can bring progress.

September 15, 2026

Learning by Building

CSS became much clearer when I stopped thinking of every element as text and started seeing it as a box. Content sits inside padding, borders surround that padding, and margins create space outside the border.

The two-column layout also showed why clearfix matters. The sidebar and content area float beside each other, while the clearfix allows their parent container to keep its full height.

September 16, 2026

Making Space to Recharge

A productive week needs moments of rest. A short walk, a conversation with friends, or an hour away from a screen can make it easier to return to work with patience and focus.

My goal for the coming week is simple: stay consistent, ask questions early, and measure progress by what I understand—not only by how quickly I finish.
